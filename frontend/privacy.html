TRUST & PRIVACY

Privacy

ZodiaDaily is designed to collect only the information needed to create and deliver a requested report.

Information you provide

This can include a name, birth date, optional second birth date, family details, gift information and checkout information when you choose to purchase.

How information is used

Birth-date details are used to generate the requested preview and personalized report. Purchase information is used to verify an eligible order and unlock the purchased report.

Payments

Checkout is handled through Shopify. ZodiaDaily does not ask you to send payment card details through the report form.

Report content

Reports may contain symbolic astrology and numerology interpretations, date-based research and AI-generated narrative. These are presented for reflection and entertainment.

Data choices

Before launch, the operator should publish the current support contact and retention/deletion process here. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the report form.
