## 6. Posting Checklist

1. Lead with the occasion/problem, not the product name.
2. Show the product interface or report page when possible.
3. Make the first sentence understandable without context.
4. Use one clear CTA.
5. Send people to the matching landing page.
6. Do not promise predictions, scientific personality results, or guaranteed compatibility.
7. Test different hooks before changing the product itself.

## 7. First-Customer Experiment

Run three content themes separately:
- Birthday gift
- Couples
- Personal discovery

Keep the same $4 offer and free preview.

Track:
- Landing-page visits
- Preview starts
- Preview completions
- Checkout clicks
- Completed purchases

Do not judge a campaign from likes alone. The useful signal is whether people move from the post to the preview and toward checkout.
